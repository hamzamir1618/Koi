01 — Creative Vision

The world KOI inhabits

KOI is not a restaurant that evokes East Asia — it inhabits it. Every visual decision draws from a specific visual vocabulary: the charged emptiness of sumi-e ink painting, the flat graphic power of Chinese woodblock prints, the ceremonial weight of imperial seal stamps. The aesthetic is not decorative. It is structural.

Ink & Restraint
Negative space is active. The ink on the page matters less than the white it surrounds. KOI's visual language breathes — it never fills every surface, and when it does, the density is intentional, not decorative noise.
The Dragon & the Koi
Chinese mythology holds that koi who swim upstream and leap the Dragon Gate become dragons. KOI's identity lives in that transformation — the aspiration, the movement against current, the moment of becoming.
Stage, Not Scene
The wave is a stage backdrop. The sakura falls like a theatre effect. KOI presents food as performance — a living composition with deliberate pacing, lighting, and a beginning, middle, and end.

02 — Colour Palette

Flat colour. No gradients.

Every color is a flat hex. No CSS gradients, no canvas gradient objects, no SVG gradient fills — anywhere. Depth is achieved through opacity layers, scale, and the spatial arrangement of flat shapes. This mirrors the flat-field aesthetic of Chinese woodblock printing and traditional porcelain painting.

03 — Typography

The written stroke

Two type systems coexist. Western type carries warmth and editorial precision; Chinese calligraphy carries mythic weight. Neither translates the other — they speak at different registers simultaneously, like an orchestra where strings and percussion share the stage without competition.

05 — Motion & Animation

Every frame earns its place

Animation is not decoration. Each motion effect is chosen for what it communicates: elastic stamps feel like a seal being pressed; character splits feel like calligraphy being written; wave parallax feels like standing at a harbour watching tides roll through depth. Motion follows Lenis smooth scroll for continuous inertia throughout.

Lenis 1.0 Smooth Scroll
GSAP 3.12 + ScrollTrigger
Canvas rAF Loops
CSS Keyframe Marquee
SVG stroke-dashoffset
Wave Parallax
chinese-wave.png drawn 3× on canvas at different heights, speeds and opacities. Back layer moves at 0.5px/frame, mid at 0.95, front at 1.6. Creates depth illusion of a real sea receding into distance.
canvas · drawImage · rAF
Pinned Wave Section
Section stays fixed for 280% of scroll height. GSAP ScrollTrigger monitors progress and fires elastic character stamps at 15%, 32%, 50%. Headline fades up at 68%. Gives cinematic dwell time inside the wave.
ScrollTrigger · pin:true · elastic.out(1,.5)
Character Stamp Reveals
水 墨 境 each drop from y:-140 at scale 1.8 with random rotation, landing with elastic.out easing. Feels like a chop/stamp being pressed onto the wave. Used for section headers and seals throughout.
fromTo · scale:1.8→1 · rotateZ → 0
Heading Split Reveal
Story heading text is split into individual characters by JS. Each character animates in with random rotation ±9° and y:60 offset, staggered 40ms. Feels like calligraphy being written one stroke at a time.
innerHTML split · back.out(1.5) · stagger:.04
Menu Stamp-In
Dish items scale from 1.25× with a slight random rotation and snap into place with back.out(1.8) — as if each menu card is being placed down on the table with a satisfying press.
scale:1.25→1 · rotateZ:random · back.out(1.8)
Experience Card Cascade
Three cards enter from different directions simultaneously — card 1 from x:-80 with rotateX:20, card 2 from y:60, card 3 from x:+80 with rotateX:20 — as if the triptych unfolds outward from a central point.
translateX/Y · rotateX:20→0 · perspective:800px
Cherry Blossom Canvas
65 petals on hero, 32 on experience section. Each Petal has independent velocity, swing oscillation, rotation, scale and one of 7 flat pink tones. Petals are either 5-petal flowers or single ellipses — same probability split as a real bloom.
canvas · Petal class · rAF · Math.sin swing
SVG Dragon Draw
Dragon SVG paths use stroke-dasharray and stroke-dashoffset animation — the dragon literally draws itself in ink over 3.8 seconds on page load. CSS animation, no JS required, with prefers-reduced-motion respect.
stroke-dashoffset:4000→0 · 3.8s cubic-bezier

06 — Graphic Elements

The clutter is the craft

Chinese visual tradition uses deliberate density. A bare surface is an unfinished thought. KOI's decorative system layers multiple elements at varying opacities to build visual richness without noise — each layer reads at its own depth.

龍鲤水道
Ghost Glyphs
Ma Shan Zheng characters at 30–80vw, 2–6% opacity, positioned as compositional anchors. Used in every section. As much illustration as typography.
Auspicious Clouds (祥云)
Traditional Chinese cloud shapes used as floating SVG elements at 6–12% opacity. Three sizes placed throughout hero and reservation sections.
鲤
福
Red Seal Stamps
Square border frames with Chinese characters at slight rotations. Used as authentic-looking 印章 (yìnzhāng) chops — a core Chinese artistic convention on paintings and documents.
Ink Splash Marks
Organic flat SVG blobs mimicking dried ink pools and splatter. Used at 18–25% opacity on the hero to reinforce the sumi-e, studio-of-a-painter atmosphere.
Lattice Border (回纹)
A CSS repeating pattern in seal red at the top and bottom of the menu section, referencing the traditional 回纹 key-fret border used on Chinese ceramics and architecture.
Cherry Blossom Branch
Hand-crafted SVG branch in flat ink strokes with flat-color 5-petal blossoms and amber stamens. Positioned in the upper-left of the hero as a compositional counterweight to the dragon on the right.
