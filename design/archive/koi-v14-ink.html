Our Story

Born from ink, flame & memory

KOI was born from a single obsession: to honour the ancient with the precise. Our kitchen traces its lineage to the imperial courts of the Tang dynasty, where food was not merely sustenance but ceremony — a living painting, a brushstroke on silk.

Every dish is composed the way a master calligrapher approaches blank paper — with patience, intention, and the understanding that restraint is the most powerful stroke of all.

The Experience

Dinner begins before you sit down.

一
The Space
Ivory silk canopies, ink-washed stone, paper lanterns hung low enough to touch. The room was designed so that nowhere you look is accidental.
二
The Ritual
Dinner begins with the ceremonial pouring of aged pu-erh and ends with handwritten fortune characters drawn at your table by the chef.
三
The Craft
The kitchen has no wall. You watch the wok smoke, the ladle arc, the knife go still. It is not a show — it is just the work, made visible.
